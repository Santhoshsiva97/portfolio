## Problem
PS Textile sells sarees, kurtis and half-saree sets that come in through WhatsApp group catalogues. Customers had no single place to browse products, see prices and order. Most of them shop on their phones, arriving from links shared on WhatsApp.

## My role
I handled the project end to end: creative direction, a static prototype for approval, a custom Online Store 2.0 theme, the product catalogue and launch preparation. The owner kept control of payments, KYC and account setup.

## Approach
- **Design first:** I built the "Woven Light" direction (silk on a rosewood table in warm lamp light) as a static prototype and got it approved before writing any Liquid.
- **A custom theme built on Shopify's Skeleton theme:** Liquid, vanilla JS web components and CSS custom properties, with no build step.
- **Motion with restraint:** GSAP and ScrollTrigger reveals inspired by saree borders, all switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Animations never delay the main image.
- **Mobile and WhatsApp first:** fast mobile pages and rich link previews for sharing.

## Key features
- A custom home page, collections and product pages in a heritage-luxury style
- Product cards that crossfade to a second photo, and a curtain-style cart drawer
- A catalogue imported from WhatsApp posts, with SEO-safe handles and descriptions
- Payment setup for Razorpay or Cashfree plus Cash on Delivery, and Tamil Nadu shipping rules
